ABOUT Aashray ESTATES

More than an address.

A Lucknow-based real estate company founded in 2018 with a focus on quality, design, trust and community.

OUR STORY

Built around a simple idea.

Founded in 2018, Aashray Estates is a Lucknow-based real estate company built around a simple idea: a property should feel like more than an address.

We create thoughtfully planned spaces where quality, design, trust and community come together.

With a deep understanding of Lucknow's evolving landscape, we look beyond the transaction to create places that people can genuinely call their own.

Our ambition is simple—to create distinctive addresses with lasting value and a lasting sense of belonging.

OUR VISION

Trusted addresses for generations.

To become one of Lucknow's most trusted and respected real estate brands, known for creating thoughtfully designed spaces, enduring communities and addresses that hold value for generations.

We aspire to shape the way people experience real estate—not simply by developing properties, but by creating places where people feel secure, connected and truly at home.

OUR MISSION

Trust, quality and long-term value.

To create thoughtfully designed spaces built on trust, quality and long-term value, where people can truly feel at home.
